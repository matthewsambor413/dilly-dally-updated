Brand note · 2026
Our founder was filling out an application for a craft fair she hoped might accept her. Somewhere in the middle of that application, she had a better idea — I'll just make my own.

Not out of bitterness—just a preference for the slow way.

Dilly Dally is a curated fair for people who value the detour. We want to showcase work that is still evolving: the prototypes, the small batches, and the pieces that took far too long to make.

We're gathering a group of designers and artists who care about the narrative of the object. If you're more interested in the integrity of the material than the speed of the sale, we'd love to see what you're working on. Let's make our own space
